What this can and can't tell you: the straightened-card centering is a real measurement, good to roughly ±2 points on a clean, sharp photo. The PSA / BGS / CGC rows show the best grade your centering would allow under each company's published (or, for BGS, commonly cited) tolerances — a ceiling, not a predicted grade. Corners, edges and surface decide the rest, and a phone photo can't judge those reliably, so there is deliberately no single "you'll get a 9" number. The corner/edge check is a rough heuristic and is inconclusive on light or white-bordered cards. The grading companies don't all publish exact numbers and sources disagree in places — treat these as guidance and confirm against each company's current standards.
